About Us

Wag Works was founded with a simple mission: give dogs the exercise, care, and joy they deserve while their humans are away.

Our walkers are trained, insured, and lifelong dog lovers. Whether you have a senior pup, a high‑energy adventurer, or a brand‑new puppy, we’re here to make their days brighter.
